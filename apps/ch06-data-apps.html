Fig. 5: The real price of industrial commodities

Hover over the line to read the index; apart from the World War I spike, prices fell for most of the century, with a trough in 2002.

Sources. Data: Jacks (2019), “From Boom to Bust: A Typology of Real Commodity Prices in the Long Run”, Cliometrica 13(2), data file “Real commodity prices, 1850–2020” (archived copy of the author’s data page), for 1900–2020; extended to 2025 with the World Bank, Commodity Price Data (The Pink Sheet), deflated by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPIAUCSL), via FRED. The textbook’s figure uses proprietary Global Financial Data, so our index is built from these open sources instead. Prepared file: data/ch06/fig6-5.csv (see the data notes).
